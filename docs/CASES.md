# Verified Reference Cases

These cases justify experiments, not guaranteed ROI. Metrics are reported by
the platform or vendor unless stated otherwise.

| Case | Reported result | Evidence limit | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Klarna marketing | 25% lower agency spend and about USD 10M annual savings | Company disclosure, not an independent audit | https://www.klarna.com/international/press/klarna-cuts-marketing-agency-spend-by-25-with-genai-saving-10m-annually/ |
| LuisaViaRoma with TikTok Symphony | 71% higher CTR, 11% lower CPA, 80% of clicks from avatar creative | TikTok advertising case, not TikTok Shop operating cost | https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/inspiration/luisaviaroma-case-study |
| Amazon listing tools | More than 500,000 selling partners used the tools by Dec 2024; nearly 90% of suggested attributes accepted with minimal or no edits | Platform aggregate; adoption is not savings | https://trustworthyshopping.aboutamazon.com/eight-ai-innovations-enhancing-trust-amazon-shopping |
| Wildride with Gorgias | 33% of email tickets automated and 24% faster resolution | Vendor customer story | https://www.gorgias.com/customers/wildride |
| Everyday Dose with Gorgias and Siena | 60% faster first response, 45% faster resolution, over 30% more tickets converted to sales | Vendor customer story without a control group | https://www.gorgias.com/customers/everyday-dose |
| Healf with Prediko | Stockout rate reportedly fell from 4% to 1% in two months | Vendor customer story without model or SKU detail | https://www.prediko.io/case-studies |
